# Agent guidance

- Follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for source, checks and documentation.
- Follow the [normal task recipe](docs/DEVELOPMENT.md#normal-task): prove and compile the exact changed leaf in the prepared workspace. Preserve caches and compatible validation across review, merge, integration and publication; missing artifacts, receipts or a phase transition do not assign a whole-library rebuild.
- At meaningful final delivery, include applicable declaration/text-style lint, affected in-package `QuillenTest` tests and basic source/no-placeholder checks, with incremental native compilation and selected batch lint. Do not automatically add downstream setup/build/lint, kernel replay, additional inventories or negative-control chains, including at merge, publication or adoption. Independent mathematical review and writing acceptance remain required under their governing contracts. Reuse completed categories after repairs. Helper returns, commits and merges do not trigger standalone lint/tests; compilation alone does not supply their separate coverage. [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#tests-and-checks) owns the commands and scope.
- For new general theory intended for Aglib, follow the
  [candidate convention](docs/DEVELOPMENT.md#aglib-candidates) before choosing an implementation
  owner; keep application adapters outside that subtree.
- Keep history, workflow, evidence/provenance, package summaries and operational
  guidance in `docs/`.
  Source-family READMEs and focused guides under `Quillen/` own mathematical
  results, API/interfaces, exact hypotheses and remaining inputs. Maintain one
  authority and update inbound links when moving content.

- Preserve [mathematical trust](docs/DEVELOPMENT.md#mathematical-trust): source correspondence, no transitive `sorry`, approved axiom closure, dependency boundaries and compatible audit evidence remain required. Existing selected-origin axiom auditing is ordinary final-validation coverage, distinct from prohibited replay/client/control chains.
