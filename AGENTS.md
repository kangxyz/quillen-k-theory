# Agent guidance

- Follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for source, checks and documentation.
- Follow the [normal task recipe](docs/DEVELOPMENT.md#normal-task): prove and compile the exact changed leaf in the prepared workspace. Preserve caches and compatible validation across review, merge, integration and publication; missing artifacts, receipts or a phase transition do not assign a whole-library rebuild.
- At meaningful final delivery, include applicable declaration/text-style lint, affected in-package `QuillenTest` tests and basic source/no-placeholder checks, through the same native build/test/lint/axiom commands as public CI; preserve compatible native artifacts and command evidence. Do not automatically add downstream setup/build/lint, kernel replay, additional inventories or negative-control chains, including at merge, publication or adoption. Independent mathematical review and writing acceptance remain required under their governing contracts. Reuse completed categories after repairs. Helper returns, commits and merges do not trigger standalone lint/tests; compilation alone does not supply their separate coverage. [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#tests-and-checks) owns the commands and scope.
- For new general theory intended for Aglib, follow the
  [candidate convention](docs/DEVELOPMENT.md#aglib-candidates) before choosing an implementation
  owner; keep application adapters outside that subtree.
- Keep history, workflow, evidence/provenance, package summaries and operational
  guidance in `docs/`.
  Source-family READMEs and focused guides under `Quillen/` own mathematical
  results, API/interfaces, exact hypotheses and remaining inputs. Maintain one
  authority and update inbound links when moving content.

- For serious compilation trouble, use the [performance lookup](docs/PERFORMANCE.md#repair-workflow) to read the relevant case before further fixes or budget changes; use upstream reference search when needed. Preserve reusable repair cases in `docs/performance/` with their conditions, failed approaches and existing evidence; update the same case for follow-ups. Keep `docs/PERFORMANCE.md` to key guidance and case routes, without deleting the underlying records when summarizing.
- Preserve [mathematical trust](docs/DEVELOPMENT.md#mathematical-trust): source correspondence, no transitive `sorry`, approved axiom closure, dependency boundaries and compatible audit evidence remain required. The pinned upstream production axiom audit is ordinary native final-check coverage, distinct from replay, client or control chains; it does not establish source correspondence.
