# Thomason–Trobaugh comparison boundaries

The constructed section completion and native current-stage localization have proved comparisons. Their identification with a specified classical Thomason–Trobaugh spectrum and its original null diagram remains open. This guide records that boundary and the separate raw-cell route; it does not rename a constructed completion as classical Kᴮ.

## Classical target and source

For qcqs `X`, an open immersion `j : U → X` with quasi-compact `U`, and `Y = X − U`, the classical target is the canonical fiber sequence

```text
Kᴮ(X on Y) → Kᴮ(X) → Kᴮ(U).
```

For a closed auxiliary support `Z` with quasi-compact complement, its supported form is

```text
Kᴮ(X on Y ∩ Z) → Kᴮ(X on Z) → Kᴮ(U on U ∩ Z).
```

R. W. Thomason and T. Trobaugh, *Higher Algebraic K-Theory of Schemes and of Derived Categories*, The Grothendieck Festschrift III, Progress in Mathematics 88 (1990), 247–435, Theorem 7.4, printed pp. 365–366; §§6.4, 6.6(a), 7.0, 7.5(a) retain the initial comparison for every `n ≥ 0`, including zero. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-0-8176-4576-2_10); [original paper](https://www.sas.rochester.edu/mth/sites/doug-ravenel/otherpapers/Thomason-Trobaugh.pdf).

## Original Laurent boundary and splitting

The positive Bass row for `K_m(S) := PerfectK(Spec S,m)` is exact for every commutative ring, including zero, and every `n ≥ 0`, including K₁ to K₀. Its chart difference is positive restriction times inverse negative restriction. Its terminal arrow uses the first O-coordinate of the same native Phi inverse after the original T-first Mayer–Vietoris boundary. The relative scalar-to-supported-cofiber theorem has positive sign because two proved inversions cancel.

The fine Laurent `[T]` terminal formula and its actual O/O(-1) zero-locus class remain open. The lower negative-chart scalar must be related to `T⁻¹`, its unit class inverted, and the original supported cone of `tautologicalLineInclusion ≫ prod.fst` compared through its positive trivialization and negative polynomial coordinate. Transport through original excision and the same native Phi inverse must determine whether the original operation gives `x` or `x⁻¹`. A natural section of the literal terminal arrow, with its proved sign, remains to be constructed. Exactness and the relative positive sign alone do not determine it. Keep all Nil contributions; classical ring-K identification, Nil vanishing, and homotopy invariance are separate.


## Original raw-cell route

coefficient bounded-flat/perfect convention and the distinct integer-base
convention on arbitrary schemes. Each keeps its original pullbacks,
normalization tracks, source null, forward unit, and independent comparison,
loop and structure coordinates.

The coefficient route has original one-step evaluation, identity and
composition cubes, and the objectwise second seed. Its selected Laurent
and polynomial source cells have their complete prescribed attachments.
The separate overlap family still needs its compatible eight three-faces,
whole intersections and source-chart attachments before descent gives the
specified second coefficient projection. Supplied chosen carriers and range
interfaces do not construct a recursive producer for every raw cell tree.

The integer-base route constructs raw scheme-arrow maps, their source-null
and unit comparisons, whole one-step evaluation naturality, and raw map
identity. `GlobalRawBass.InputIdentitySeedSquare.square` also constructs the
prescribed higher square for the chosen transition-input identity homotopy
and fixed whole raw map identity.
`GlobalBassEvaluation.InputSeedNaturality.comparison` constructs the original
`longRoute.RelHomotopy shortRoute` from all five native faces and complete
whole-word joins. `GlobalRawBass.InputCompositionSpan.homotopy` gives the
actual polynomial-span composition, including its chart pauses. The
terminal-column, row and seam identities construct the full second H1 half;
the first/second join, remaining external seams and complete cone/step
composition cube remain open. Higher coherence of the separately chosen
`InputNull.originalNullComparison` and coherent all-stage iteration retain
their own targets.

A literal prespectrum-valued functor and strict transformations are inputs
to `strictDiagramIterate`. Homotopy identity and composition data do not
satisfy that interface by themselves. Likewise, equality after evaluation
does not identify dependent scalar reference nodes or a prescribed
midpoint. A comparison through the raw route must cover every required
ambient, open and auxiliary-supported stage with its actual transition and
whole null. These obligations do not retract the internal current-stage
or normalized-row localization theorems.

The supported raw construction fixes the original integer-base charts on
qc-ambient open pairs. For an open immersion `j : U ⟶ X` with compact
`U,X` and arbitrary `T : X.Opens`, its three vanishing opens are
`T ⊔ j.opensRange`, `T`, and `j ⁻¹ᵁ T`. Its split geometric tracks,
original overlap pushout, parameter-looped cone and forward Laurent seed
are objectwise constructions, without compactness of `T`, quasi-separatedness,
flatness or an extra Cartesian premise. Categorical triple-pullback
associativity preserves the actual supported arrows but does not provide
relative R2 pastings or raw functoriality. Raw row maps, evaluation/seed
naturality and the original whole localization-null comparison remain to
be constructed.

The canonical localization `fullNull` itself has a finite directed weak-zero
realization, with exact null recovery and target-isomorphism postcomposition.
Its weak zero-to-composite edge must not be replaced by an isomorphism node.
Generic path-family extension extends an actual carrier-valued boundary;
it does not lift an arbitrary native family or supply its attachments.
Generic fiber-lift naturality still takes the complete null square `Q`.
These supplied primitives therefore do not constitute an all-successor
recipe or the supported raw same-null comparison.

## Classical object and same-null correspondence

Specify the classical model by the original raw perfect/bounded-flat-perfect
spectrum, native polynomial/Laurent squares, original cone/loop stages and
forward Laurent transitions, followed by their homotopy colimit (TT 6.4).
The forward operation must be the specified Laurent-unit cup action followed
by cone boundary, with its original functorial homotopies. The independent
returned tensor comparisons and their iterated strictifications provide
concrete inputs; their equality with every required classical raw forward
leg still needs the actual target correspondence.

If an ordinary colimit is used, apply the cofibration-replacement and
sequentialization comparison to this identified diagram. A theorem about
an arbitrary supplied strict Model diagram and track cannot substitute for
that application. The integer-parameter Model projection isomorphisms alone
also do not identify all raw stages or their transitions. Original class
maps must agree with the selected spectrum maps in every nonnegative and
negative degree.

For `T = X − Z` and `Y = X − U`, the classical auxiliary diagram has objects
on `(X,Y∩Z)`, `(X,Z)`, `(U,U∩Z)`, whose acyclicity opens are `U∪T`, `T`,
`U∩T`. Internal section/current/normalized comparisons already retain these
three rows, original enlargement/restriction and the same whole selected
null. Under qcqs `X` and quasi-compact `U,T`, a classical identification must
add comparisons to the **specified classical** three objects and its
canonical null, preserving the complete diagram.

Only then may its actual stable fiber lift and boundary be transported. In
each signed source degree `d`, the required boundary square is

```text
c_support,d−1 ∘ δ_native,d = δ_classical,d ∘ c_open,d.
```

The maps `c` must be the required original-class comparisons. The known
single-minus formulas under the fixed positive perfect classes must be
reconciled with the chosen classical boundary convention; no unsigned
square follows merely from exactness. Defining a conjugate boundary without
identifying the classical null diagram does not prove this correspondence.
The basic case takes `Z = X`.

State the library's actual stable fiber-sequence notion: all-signed stability
is not a levelwise homotopy-equivalence assertion. The final comparison
requires the classical object, forward-transition, class and whole-null
correspondence together. Classical Laurent splitting, Nil vanishing,
homotopy invariance and nonconnective descent are additional statements
unless supplied by their own identified arguments.
