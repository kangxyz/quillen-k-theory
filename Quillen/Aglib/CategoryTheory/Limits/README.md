# Displayed colimits and pushout interchange

`FinitePosetColimits` constructs principal-ideal cocones and glues two lower
ideals of a poset along their displayed overlap and pushout. Its functorial
version retains the specified preserved colimits. `PointwisePushoutInterchange`
transports displayed pushout squares through a pointwise pushout of diagrams.
Neither interface replaces these local conditions by global cocompleteness.

The declarations are generic category theory, independent of K-theory models.
Their existing namespaces and actual universal-property maps are preserved.
