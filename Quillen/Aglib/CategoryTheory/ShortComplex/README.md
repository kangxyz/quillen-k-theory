# Natural short-complex operations

NaturalSequence constructs literal pointwise short complexes from two natural transformations
with zero composite, transports them through additive whiskering/mapping, and retains original
full-subcategory lifts. MapComposition transports original short exactness of two successive
maps to the same composite functor. All category, abelian/additive and zero-map hypotheses
remain on their original statements.
