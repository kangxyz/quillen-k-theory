# Ordinary Serre quotient and slice-kernel prerequisites

`Annihilation` retains the exact annihilated-object criterion for additive Serre
localizations and the native abelian structure of the canonical quotient.
`Lifts` constructs actual isomorphism lifts through a localization, proves
filteredness/cofilteredness under the stated fraction calculus, and retains
Serre subobject/quotient lifting, subobject lattice preservation, and actual
lifted intervals with quotient comparison maps.

`OverKernel` realizes the ordinary slice kernel as pullback along zero, with its
actual maps and cartesian squares. `OverKernelBaseChange` and
`OverKernelPostcompose` retain the original kernel comparisons and natural maps.
`KernelPushout`, `KernelPushoutComparison`, and `KernelPushoutMonomorphism`
retain actual extension pushouts, kernel/cokernel universal properties,
comparison isomorphisms, and their original epi/mono hypotheses.
`ImageSubobjects` retains ordinary image and Serre-subobject statements.

Source/target universes and original declaration namespaces are unchanged.
The actual Q-valued auxiliary categories, Q/comma/span comparisons, topological
contractions and K/G localization sequence stay downstream.
