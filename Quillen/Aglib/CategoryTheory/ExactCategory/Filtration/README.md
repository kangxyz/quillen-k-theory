# Finite exact filtrations

`Basic` retains the actual finite conflation tower, its zero-object base, factors,
length, and the sum formula for conflation-additive invariants. `Hypothesis`
records objectwise existence, with no common length bound. `Functor`,
`Subcategory`, and `Refinement` preserve the actual maps under exact functors,
subquotient-closed abelian inclusions, and pullback splicing.

`Reflector` and `KernelIteration` use an epimorphic natural unit in an abelian
category; eventual vanishing of its actual iterated kernels supplies a finite
filtration. The adjunction statements retain their faithful/right-adjoint,
balancedness and quotient-closure hypotheses. `CentralEndomorphism` and
`FiniteCentralFamily` use objectwise nilpotence of actual natural endomorphisms;
no exponent uniform in all objects is assumed.

`Layers`, `LayerFunctors`, `LayerQuotients`, and `Diamond` retain ordinary
subquotient layers, their ordered supports and quotient universal properties.
`PullbackFlag` and `FlagExtension` keep finite admissible flags, actual pullbacks,
zero prefixes, and appended arrows. Original declaration namespaces are retained.
Grothendieck classes and Q/K/classifying-space endpoints remain downstream.
