# Finite admissible resolutions

`Finite` retains finite towers of actual conflations, all syzygies and resolving
terms, length, Euler expressions, presentations, and exact-functor transport.
The resolution hypothesis is objectwise finite, with no uniform length bound.
`Transport` changes only the augmentation through an isomorphism of its target.

`Closure`, `Presentation`, and `Strata` prove the original closure and
presentation-independence statements using admissible pullbacks. Repleteness,
extension/kernel closure and zero/product hypotheses stay attached to the
statements that use them. `SubcategoryComparison` retains ordinary nested exact
inclusions and their exact equivalence. `BoundedStrata` gives exact inclusions
and the actual adjacent length-one resolution under the global finite-resolution
hypothesis. `Covers` retains admissible cover objects and pullback refinements.

Original namespaces, maps and universe bounds are retained. Q categories,
classifying-space contractions, Grothendieck classes and K-map equivalences
remain in the application owners.
