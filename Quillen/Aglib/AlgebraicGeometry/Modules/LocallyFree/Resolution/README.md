# Resolution candidates

The ordinary resolution property gives vector-bundle quotients of coherent sheaves. Affine finite projective dimension gives finite locally free resolutions. Chart-module bounds and actual global syzygies give global resolutions on quasi-compact regular schemes with that resolution property. Bounds may depend on the sheaf; no global projectivity of vector bundles is asserted. Small models retain the original resolutions and lengths.

These local general-theory candidates preserve the existing declaration namespaces, hypotheses and universes. Their K-theory applications remain in downstream modules.
