# LocallyFree candidates

Finite locally free sheaves on arbitrary schemes are stalkwise flat and closed under the stated ambient kernels. Tensor and restriction operations retain their original exactness and functoriality. The coherent inclusion and coherent kernel closure retain local noetherianity. Categorical exact small models use universe u + 1.

These local general-theory candidates preserve the existing declaration namespaces, hypotheses and universes. Their K-theory applications remain in downstream modules.
