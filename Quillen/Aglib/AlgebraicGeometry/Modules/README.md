# Module sheaves and complexes

The families below use actual module sheaves on schemes and their existing
restriction, pullback, direct-image, tensor and cohomological maps. General
interfaces retain their precise finiteness, local Noetherianity, flatness,
acyclicity and boundedness conditions.

- [Quasicoherent sheaves](QuasiCoherent/README.md),
  [coherent sheaves](Coherent/README.md), and
  [finite locally free sheaves](LocallyFree/README.md).
- [Ordinary complexes](Complex/README.md),
  [derived-category functors](DerivedCategory/README.md), and
  [perfect complexes](PerfectComplexes/README.md).
- [Restriction](Restriction/README.md) and
  [scalar actions](Scalar/README.md).

`UnitProperties` proves that the structure-module unit is quasicoherent and
finite locally free on every scheme, and records the actual global-section
comparison along open immersions. Its historic projective-line namespace is
preserved during staging; the statements impose no projective-line hypothesis.
Waldhausen and K-theory constructions remain outside this candidate family.
