# Actual support of perfect complexes

Support means genuine acyclicity after the specified open restriction. Strict and local support
predicates retain their full chain categories and original zero/isomorphism/extension/shift/cone/
kernel closure. Restrictions, weakening of the acyclicity open, inverse-image and union pair
squares, supported extension by zero and its actual adjunction preserve the original maps.

Restriction vanishing and cover detection do not require perfection, quasicoherence or separation
when their displayed statements do not. Supported excision uses the stated cover by the open
image and auxiliary acyclicity open. Derived excision retains its original supported source.
All K-theoretic fibration, spectrum and group interfaces stay downstream.
