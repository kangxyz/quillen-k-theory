# Bounded-flat perfect complexes and ordinary sections

The full chain category retains local perfection, an explicit upper term bound and stalkwise-
flat terms. Actual shifts, cones, arbitrary geometric pullback, support, inherited short exact
sequences, and the original identity/composition/unit comparisons give its geometric pseudofunctor.
Replacement results retain their original cohomological bound or quasi-compactness assumption.

Affine/full-open and joint scheme/coefficient cocartesian sections retain the actual original
fibers, hom universes, strict coslice reindexing, evaluation equivalences, supported restrictions
and scalar/unit actions. Auxiliary and fiber support are the stated actual acyclicity opens.
Chosen Waldhausen zeros, model structures, prespectra, Bass seeds/towers/telescopes, quotient
gradings and their normalization/coherence protocols stay downstream.
