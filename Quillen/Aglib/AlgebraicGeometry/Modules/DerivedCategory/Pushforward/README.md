# Pushforward candidates

Actual termwise sheaf-complex direct image has no exactness or perfection assumption. Acyclic single sheaves, affine quasicoherent sheaves and flasque sheaves have the stated derived-unit comparisons. Bounded-complex results keep their original bounds. Flat morphisms preserve injective sheaves and give derived Hom transport at lower-bounded injective models.

These local general-theory candidates preserve the existing declaration namespaces, hypotheses and universes. Their K-theory applications remain in downstream modules.
