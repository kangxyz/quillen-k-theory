# Homotopy pullbacks of cochain complexes

`Basic` is the literal mapping cocone of the signed difference of the two
original maps. It retains strict projections and the specified chain homotopy,
with degreewise sections rather than an asserted chain section. In an abelian
category, `QuasiIso` identifies the homology pullback under the stated preceding
epimorphism and proves the weak-leg and gluing results for these original maps.
Object and morphism universes remain independent.
