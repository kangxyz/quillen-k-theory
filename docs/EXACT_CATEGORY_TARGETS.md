# Exact-category theorem contracts

Primary-source audit: 2026-10-08. These are newly requested formalization goals, not completed theorems. General exact-category Q/K/functoriality already exists; the completed dévissage endpoint remains the small abelian version.

## 1. Selected exact localization contract

Reference: Marco Schlichting, *Delooping the K-theory of exact categories* (2004), [author manuscript](https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/sci/maths/people/staff/marco_schlichting/research/delooptopology.pdf), Definitions 1.3, 1.5, 1.12–1.15; Proposition 1.16; Theorems 2.1 and 2.10 (PDF pp. 2–4, 8, 10).

Let U be a small exact category and A a full, replete, extension-closed subcategory, with inherited exact structure. For the right s-filtering version require:

1. Closure under admissible subobjects and admissible quotients in U.
2. Every map U → A to an object of A factors through a deflation U ↠ B with B in A.
3. Every inflation A ↪ U with A in the subcategory admits a deflation U ↠ B with B in A such that the composite A → B is an inflation.

The left version is dual: maps A → U factor through inflations B ↪ U, and deflations U ↠ A admit inflations B ↪ U with composite B ↠ A a deflation.

Weak isomorphisms are finite composites of inflations with cokernel in A and deflations with kernel in A. The exact quotient U/A is their categorical localization; its conflations are sequences isomorphic to images of U-conflations. Proposition 1.16 constructs this exact structure without assuming A idempotent complete.

Theorem 2.1 additionally assumes A idempotent complete and gives the homotopy fibration of Quillen K-spaces

`K(A) → K(U) → K(U/A)`, with `K(E) = Ω BQ(E)`.

Only A, not U or U/A, is required idempotent complete. Formalizing this is a genuine new quotient/exact-structure and localization theorem. Do not label it a BQ-level fiber theorem without proving the needed delooping/comparison.

The connective K-group sequence must use the existing canonical KMap. Ordinary K₀(U) → K₀(U/A) is surjective because the quotient keeps the objects of U and algebraic Grothendieck K₀ is generated by object classes; this deduction must be connected to the genuine homotopical K₀ comparison. The resulting connective tail ends at K₀(U/A) → 0.

Nonconnective localization is separate. In Schlichting §2.9, π₀ IK(E) is K₀(E^idem), while positive groups agree with Quillen K-groups. Theorem 2.10 cannot simply be appended to the current ordinary-K₀ tail without this degree-zero comparison. Essentially-small/universe transport is also separate.

## 2. Selected sufficient exact dévissage baseline

Reference: Quillen, *Higher algebraic K-theory I*, §3 Theorem 2 and Corollary 2, printed pp. 105–107, [original paper](https://www.sas.rochester.edu/mth/sites/doug-ravenel/otherpapers/Quillen-Higher-I.pdf#page=21).

Name the target **functorial exact dévissage**, rather than unrestricted objectwise dévissage. For a fully exact inclusion i:A → E require a finite filtration of exact endofunctors

`0 ≅ F₀ ⇒ F₁ ⇒ ⋯ ⇒ Fᵣ ≅ id_E`

and exact functors Gⱼ:E → A with natural conflations

`Fⱼ₋₁(X) ↪ Fⱼ(X) ↠ i(Gⱼ(X))`.

Require the restricted filtration along i to lift to a filtration by exact endofunctors of A and actual A-conflations. The lift must be constructed or supplied as explicit categorical filtration data; it is not a K-equivalence assumption.

Quillen additivity yields the inverse sum of graded K-maps in every nonnegative degree. Both composite identities arise from the two actual functorial filtrations. This requires a proved general exact-category additivity theorem and canonical-map/group-operation compatibility. It does not follow merely by choosing a finite filtration separately for each object. No completion claim is made yet.

## 3. Broader objectwise exact dévissage remains a contract-research goal

No source was verified asserting arbitrary exact-category higher dévissage from only objectwise finite admissible filtrations and admissible-subquotient closure. Do not silently convert the completed abelian statement by deleting `Abelian`.

Verified broader frameworks have additional, substantive conditions:

- [Raptis, Theorem 5.7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1811.09564), PDF p. 21: good, admissible Waldhausen pairs with a dévissage condition. Goodness includes cylinder and two-out-of-three structure; dévissage filters morphisms after weak-equivalence replacement. Its admissibility (Definition 4.1) is a specific K-equivalence condition, not ordinary admissibility of exact-category arrows. The current project must prove that condition in a concrete model, not use it as the desired theorem in disguise.
- [Campbell–Zakharevich, Theorem 6.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1811.08014), PDF pp. 18–20: objectwise filtration dévissage for full pre-ACGW subcategories with subobject/quotient closure and creation of restricted pushouts. Definition 5.5 adds genuine intersection, mixed-square, and union/quotient compatibility. Exact categories provide CGW structures automatically, not all the stronger hypotheses.
- [Brüstle–Hassoun–Tattar](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2006.03505), Definition 5.7/Theorem 5.11: a Diamond axiom gives Jordan–Hölder, not a higher-K theorem. Theorem 1.4 (3.7/4.22) also shows that globally imposing their admissible intersection/sum conditions recovers abelian categories with maximal exact structure. Such conditions must not hide the old abelian scope under new terminology.
