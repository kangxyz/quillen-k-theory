## Global proof obligation and route assessment

[Bousfield–Friedlander, Theorem B.4](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/files/BousfieldFriedlanderSpectra.pdf)

right-hand rows are connected. In this application those rows are actual
inner S-realizations. The concrete row product comparison above must first
supply the row fiber square; it is not an assumed target conclusion.

replacement package, or proved singular-Kan bridge. Existing Theorem B proves
actual replacement projection fiber comparisons under genuine transition

presentation, its exact forward-map identification, and its transition
hypotheses are constructed. The current comma-specific Grothendieck module

The remaining global step therefore requires either a specialized
connected-row diagonal proof or a new genuine replacement presentation.
The first two layers make the product and connectedness obligations concrete
and independently checkable while that choice is developed. No conditional
wrapper assuming the requested global fiber equivalence will be introduced.
